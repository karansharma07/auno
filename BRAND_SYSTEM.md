# AUNO — Brand and experience architecture (working identity)

> **AUNO is a provisional name.** This document is a creative and product-naming system, **not** trademark, domain, app-store, or Arabic-language clearance. Commission appropriate UAE and international checks before committing the legal brand or spending on production identity.

## The mother brand

**AUNO** — the people-powered city companion.

**Promise:** Find your people. Make your plans. Experience your city.

**Master line:** **Life, together.**

**City-first line:** **Your city. Your people. Your next experience.**

**Guiding principle:** Real-world belonging is the product; technology makes it easier.

### Brand personality

- Human, curious, optimistic, warm — never juvenile.
- Premium but inclusive — not an exclusive members' club.
- Confidently useful — never magical promises or misleading AI certainty.
- Safe and clear — verification, pricing, custody and social boundaries are explained precisely.
- A balanced cultural voice for residents, newcomers, visitors and Dubai's many languages.

## One brand, eight interconnected experiences

**AUNO Now** — Instant intent-to-action discovery; how much time, where, what mood and budget.

**AUNO Together** — Recurring small-group circles and real-world connections.

**AUNO Passport** — Personal record of verified attendance milestones and self-curated city exploration. Not a travel credential or cash-value token.

**AUNO Concierge** — Saved, customisable city itineraries with realistic time/budget constraint handling after routing provider integration.

**AUNO Local Lens** — Community-curated places and contextual recommendations, with moderation and clear source labels.

**AUNO Homebase** — Welcome and settling-in companion for newcomers, linking to official sources for regulated information.

**AUNO HostOS** — Community organiser operating toolkit: event programmes, templates, membership and attendance analytics.

**AUNO for Business** — Hotel, workplace, university and residential community programmes; controlled B2B lead and contract process.

**AUNO Life** — The discovery hub tying the experiences together. This is a navigation term, not a ninth paid product.

**Not eight disconnected apps:** one sign-in, shared interests, a common events/community graph, saved places, participation history and privacy settings. Role-specific interfaces expose additional tools when permitted.

**Logo direction:** lower-case wordmark `auno.` with a distinct friendly geometric letter `a`; use soft-rectangular app-mark shapes, an accent dot, and balanced whitespace. Do not copy existing brand marks. Commission scalable vector lockups and contrast/a11y QA before public marketing.

**Typography:** system-first interface stack for performance with a distinctive editorial heading treatment: generous size, tight display tracking, restrained body copy and consistently readable labels. Add Arabic type pairing and RTL layouts before wider UAE marketing.

**Motion:** fast, purposeful, reduced-motion respectful. Small confirmation feedback; no autoplay hijacking, long loader animations, or endless feeds.

**Illustration:** original place-inspired vector art; people-first photography only with consent and appropriate usage rights. Do not use fabricated photographs as evidence of real events.

## Naming rules

1. Use **AUNO + short descriptive word** for consumer experiences; consistent title case.
2. Use **AUNO HostOS** and **AUNO for Business** for business products (different buyers, same parent brand).
3. Never use "verified" unless the exact verification assertion is documented, up-to-date and scoped.
4. Don't create a separate loyalty currency. Passport stamps and points are initially non-cash, non-transferable.
5. Avoid trademark usage of other products and secure proper consent for partner branding.
6. Conduct **trademark, company registration, domains, Arabic linguistic, social handle and app-store** clearance before launch.

## Navigation hierarchy

- Member: Home, AUNO Now, **AUNO Life hub**, Discover, People, Communities, Journeys, Rewards, Profile.
- From AUNO Life: Together, Passport, Concierge, Local Lens, Homebase (plus a view of HostOS and for Business).
- Approved host: Host Studio + HostOS; existing community/events tools remain first class.
- Business partner: Partner Studio + AUNO for Business.
- Operator: Mission Control + Business pilot reviews + content safety/approvals.

## Trust language

Show "Demo" prominently on the public prototype. Avoid claiming that localStorage simulation is shared or secured. `Passport` is achievements only, **not** an official travel document. "Concierge" must show routing and opening-hour uncertainty. "HostOS" is not a permit or a substitute for a trained professional organiser. Business forms on the demo collect invented organisations only.
