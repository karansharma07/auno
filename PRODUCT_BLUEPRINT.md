# AUNO — Product master blueprint

## 1. Venture thesis

**Working name:** AUNO (pending brand clearance)  
**Category:** trusted, people-powered city companion and community operating system  
**Launch city:** Dubai, UAE  
**North Star:** repeat real-world participation and verified meaningful experiences, not screen time

### Vision
Build the world's most trusted network of real-world communities, beginning in Dubai.

### Mission
Make it effortless, safe and rewarding for residents and visitors to discover people, build belonging and experience cities together.

### Brand promises
- **People before feeds:** fewer empty interactions, more shared experiences.
- **Trust by design:** verified identity systems, accountable hosts, transparent processes and genuine reporting.
- **Experiences we stand behind:** recurring formats, venue standards, quality controls and active support.
- **One identity across a city:** person, place, itinerary, community, booking and reward context work together.
- **Useful when you need it:** travel assistance and local services, not merely a social graph.

## 2. Audiences and jobs to be done

| Platform trust team | Maintain safe, high-quality operations | Review approvals → monitor alerts → resolve reports → audit actions |

## 3. Product map

### Member product
1. **AUNO Home** — context-aware resident and visitor welcome; personal recommendations.
2. **Discover** — events, sports, places and communities; searchable catalog, filters, maps (future).
3. **People** — member discovery, shared interests and mutual connection requests; opt-in visibility (production gate).
4. **Moments** — place-linked stories, community knowledge and contextual sharing, never an endless engagement feed.
5. **Circles** — verified micro-communities, approvals, member roles, events, announcements and governance.
6. **Journeys** — itinerary creation by destination, travel duration, interests, budget, pace, social intent and existing reservations.
7. **AUNO Stow** — storage marketplace with inventory, booking, tagging, check-in/out, liability and partner settlement.
8. **Social Plans** — member-proposed public or private group activities; capacity, approval and intent-based matching.
9. **AUNO Play** — sports bookings, recurring sessions, teams, leagues, competition scoring and sportsmanship recognition.
10. **Rewards** — XP for checked-in participation, approved contribution, milestones and non-cash partner benefits.
11. **Inbox + WhatsApp** — member communications, opted-in messages, event reminders, customer support and invitation deep links.
12. **Profile and Identity** — verification tier, privacy preferences, interests, milestones, participation and connections.

### Host and community product
Approved host accounts, team roles, member-review queues, event publication, venue/permit checklist, capacity and pricing, attendance scanning, refunds/escalations, seasonal leagues, standings, revenue dashboards, community health analytics and compliance review.

### Partner product
Business onboarding, location/space inventory, operating hours, pricing, blackout dates, booking slots, custody receipts, signed handoff, collection identity check, cancellation/incident management, settlements, insurance metadata, audit trail, partner quality metrics.

### Operations product
Member verification queue, moderation case management, audit trails, community approvals, host accreditation, partner due diligence, activity review, payment disputes, fraud detection, notification templates, promotions and coupons, content moderation, service health, analytics, role provisioning, compliance oversight and multi-city configuration.

## 4. Core differentiation (the AUNO Standard)

1. **Trust isn't a badge:** independent identity checks, consent and member conduct/accountability are separate systems.
2. **Recurring rituals:** social circles and weekly activities measured by repeat attendance and familiar faces.
3. **Experience quality:** host playbooks, venue suitability, clear event details, reliability, support and feedback.
4. **People + places + time + intent:** the personalisation engine makes *actionable* city plans rather than static recommendation lists.
5. **City utility:** luggage storage, local businesses and itinerary timing become part of the social experience.
6. **CommunityOS:** approved groups use real operational tools rather than relying on informal group chats alone.
7. **Shared reputation:** participation and helpful contribution (not popularity or time spent scrolling) shape rewards.

## 5. Recommended UX structure

**Primary mobile navigation:** Home, Discover, Journeys, Circles, You.  
Secondary contextual routes: People, Moments, Activities, Stow, Inbox, Rewards.  
**Resident and Visitor** are view preferences, not separate accounts. The identity and reward ledger remain shared.

### Critical journeys with acceptance criteria

**J1 — First event**
- First-time member discovers an event by skill, date, location and price.
- Details show organiser identity, event venue, inclusivity policy, cancellation conditions and remaining capacity.
- Member books (real payment provider required for paid events), receives confirmation and reminder.
- Host verifies attendance; reward accrues only on validated participation.
- Member receives an accessible post-event follow-up and invitations to recurring sessions.

**J2 — Create a community**
- Founder completes the application and declarations.
- Operations team reviews identity, purpose and safety obligations.
- Approved founder configures community membership rules, host team and recurring activities.
- Unapproved communities cannot publish public events.
- Appeals and review history are auditable.

**J3 — Discover people and make a plan**
- Profiles are only searchable by explicit opt-in.
- No exact-location or phone-number exposure.
- Connection requests must be mutual before one-on-one conversations.
- Group-first activities provide an alternative to random direct messaging.
- Blocking, reporting and incident follow-up are always available.

**J4 — Travel itinerary**
- Member provides destination, arrival/departure, accommodation/starting area, constraints, interests and budget.
- Planner combines trusted local knowledge, partner businesses, scheduled activities, opening times and transport durations.
- Member can review assumptions, reorder or remove stops, reserve activities and share with a group.
- Route stays within feasible time and cost constraints; confidence and data freshness are explicit.

**J5 — Luggage storage**
- Member discovers *approved* storage sites with actual hours, capacity, location, price, insurance and prohibited-item policy.
- Booking does not oversell capacity, including overlapping date/time windows.
- Host verifies drop-off, applies unique bag tags, takes appropriate condition records and issues receipt.
- Pickup needs proof, retrieval status and an auditable chain of custody.
- Delay, loss/damage, dispute, refunds and settlement paths must exist before real operations.

## 6. Launch strategy

**Beachhead:** recurring badminton and padel sessions organised to a high standard, across a constrained number of Dubai locations.  
**Community wedge:** verified founding community leaders and their recurring groups.  
**Travel expansion:** visitor mode, curated itinerary planning, resident-meets-visitor activities.  
**Storage expansion:** start by integrating appropriate existing licensed storage operators; evaluate café/restaurant hosting only following regulatory, insurance and premises assessment.

## 7. Non-goals for initial public release

Tradable cryptocurrency, open unreviewed public event hosting, unmoderated anonymous messaging, unrestricted location broadcasting, automated visa/immigration advice, unlicensed guided tours, direct luggage custody by AUNO without appropriate permissions, and a generic algorithmic entertainment feed.

## 8. Healthy platform metrics

- Verified onboarding completion and optional discovery consent.
- First-event participation rate.
- Confirmed attendance, cancellation and no-show rates.
- Repeat participation (30/60/90-day).
- Recurring-circle retention and organiser reliability.
- Helpful connection acceptance and group participation (privacy-safe aggregates).
- Host quality, member satisfaction and incident response.
- Itinerary satisfaction and realistic completion, not just generation.
- Partner storage fill rate, bag loss/damage incidence, payout accuracy.
- Net revenue, contribution margin and acquisition efficiency by cohort.

## 9. Open founder decisions

- Working brand (AUNO) clearance and registered company identity.
- Launch target segment / neighbourhood density.
- Paid versus free sessions and initial pricing boundaries.
- Verification requirements per member/host/partner role.
- Whether AUNO is principal event operator or marketplace (and where this varies).
- Storage agency vs custody model and insurance responsibilities.
- Loyalty points legal classification, sponsorship economics and restrictions.
- English-first vs English-and-Arabic launch scope.
