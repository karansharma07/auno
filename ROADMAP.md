# AUNO — Execution roadmap and definition of done

## Strategy: launch narrow, architect wide

The ambition is the ultimate resident-and-visitor city companion. The initial market wedge should remain **recurring, high-trust sports and community gatherings**. The second wedge expands into visitor discovery and group experiences. Luggage storage follows only with approved operators and clear liability.

### Stage 0 — Founder decisions and evidence

Deliverables:
- Brand shortlist, UAE trademark/domain/app clearance (AUNO provisional).
- Competitive feature matrix, mystery shopping and interviews with local community founders, sports organisers, members, visitors and hotels.
- Two neighbourhood launch zones, two launch sports, founding venues and anchor organisers.
- Regulatory advice: sports events, tourism, marketplace, storage, personal data and loyalty.
- Proposed company operating model, responsibilities and initial unit economics.

Definition of done: founders can state exactly **who joins first**, **why they switch**, **what gets booked**, **who is responsible for safety**, **who earns what**, and **how we know it works**.

### Stage 1 — Community core (MVP)

**Products:** member experience, organiser studio, internal operations.

Epics:
1. Production identity + onboarding + privacy controls.
2. Interests and approachable event/community discovery.
3. Approved circle lifecycle + member admission.
4. Event publish, capacity, real booking, payments and policies.
5. Host attendance checks, cancellation/refund and communication.
6. Trust & Safety reporting and operator tooling.
7. Event analytics and recurring-circle retention.
8. WhatsApp Business opt-in messages for confirmations/reminders where supported.

Acceptance: a new verified member can complete an attended event with a verified host and a clear support path; no unsafe or unauthorised event bypasses operations.

### Stage 2 — People + social travel

**Products:** people discovery, Moments, social plans, Journeys, Visitor mode.

Epics:
1. Opt-in people discovery, matching based on *interest and shared intent*, mutual requests and moderation.
2. Social plans with group size, eligibility, organiser approval and chat/updates.
3. Place-linked Moments, recommendations and contextual community content.
4. Journey planner with real opening hours, route durations, booking windows, budget and accessibility constraints.
5. Operator/partner tours and ticket inventory.
6. Collaborative itineraries and sharing.

Acceptance: a visitor with a defined stay can build a feasible itinerary, meet members through approved activities and verify the real experience before booking.

### Stage 3 — AUNO Stow and city utilities

**Products:** partner registration, storage inventory, member reservation, staff custody app and finance.

Epics:
1. Licensed/approved pilot partners; site audit and contract workflow.
2. Accurate inventory, opening hours, blackout dates, bag dimensions and booking concurrency.
3. Payment capture, settlement and refund policy.
4. Check-in/out, unique bag tagging, custody audit trail and evidence safeguards.
5. Insurance/claims, lost/damaged/late collections and on-call partner support.
6. Connection to Journeys with airport-buffer/route-aware itinerary optimisation.

Acceptance: every paid bag has an accountable operator, accepted liability, valid booking, safe handover and documented retrieval; inventory cannot be oversold.

### Stage 4 — CommunityOS and sustainable growth

**Products:** leagues, competitions, host CRM, venue partners, premium benefits and community insights.

Epics:
1. Skill-aware sports sessions, leagues and team competition formats.
2. Fair XP ledger, fraud-resistant achievements and non-cash partner rewards.
3. Host revenue tools and quality dashboards.
4. Community branches/chapters and cross-community experiences.
5. B2B/corporate and residential programmes.
6. Referral loops focused on attendance, not registrations.

Acceptance: repeat participation improves; members and hosts see clear utility; unit economics remain healthy; trust and safety metrics remain within agreed thresholds.

### Stage 5 — Multi-city intelligence

Add destination expansion only after city-by-city operating readiness: legal reviews, place data, regional support, host density, local partnerships and multilingual content. Build local networks, not empty geographic pins.

## The first 90 days: operational validation, not a production promise

- **Days 1–30:** 30–50 member interviews, 10+ organiser/venue interviews, validate name, legally map events and storage, test offline concept and two repeat weekly sports formats.
- **Days 31–60:** recruit anchor organisers, standardise event playbooks, measure booking/attendance/no-show/repeat rates, validate willingness to pay, start high-fidelity UX and production architecture.
- **Days 61–90:** run closed, consented prototype pilot for members/hosts, integrate an appropriate PSP + real auth in a production branch, operate trust desk, establish initial KPIs and go/no-go gates.

**Targets above are planning hypotheses, not market forecasts or guaranteed timelines.** Funding, team size, permits, integrations and validated customer needs determine sequencing.

## Priority production backlog

**P0** — Authentication, authorisation, consent, real bookings/payments, platform safety, permits, verified venues, monitoring, accessible onboarding.  
**P1** — Host workflows, recurring circles, people matching, Moments, itinerary data, reminders.  
**P2** — Storage partners after compliance, XP/benefits, premium subscriptions, leagues, city-wide event operations.  
**P3** — Advanced AI concierge, multi-city, corporate CommunityOS, tokenised experiments *only if separately approved*.

## Decision dashboard

**North Star:** verified members who participate in >=2 real-world activities per rolling 30 days.  
**Guardrails:** incidents per 1,000 attended events; check-in fraud; storage claims; partner cancellations; unresolved cases; refund SLA; booking overcapacity.  
**Business:** unit contribution margin by event/storage, venue-partner retention, repeat attendance, referral conversion, organic host activation.
